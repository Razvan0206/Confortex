# Niche: trades and local services (trades-local-services)

Status: **draft, unverified**. Plumbers, electricians, HVAC, builders, roofers, cleaners, movers, gardeners.

## Who and why they visit

Someone with a need today or this week, comparing a few local providers by phone speed and trust. Mobile, search-driven ("instalator <city>"). They decide in seconds: do you cover my area, can you come, roughly how much, can I trust you.

## Primary conversion

Primary: **call now** (tap-to-call everywhere) and WhatsApp with photos of the problem. Secondary: request a quote, see services and areas.

## Section order

1. Hero: what you do + area, huge call button, "intervenții rapide" only if true, availability/hours.
2. Servicii (concrete list; icons are own SVGs).
3. Zone deservite (cities/neighborhoods; helps local SEO).
4. Cum lucrăm: call, assessment, estimate, work, payment.
5. Lucrări realizate (real photos, before/after with permission).
6. Despre: who you are, years in business (true), authorizations/certifications shown if provided.
7. Recenzii (verbatim, approved).
8. Contact: phone, WhatsApp (invite photos), hours incl. urgent availability.

## Collect

Services and what is not offered; service area; hours and emergency policy; price approach (hourly, per job, free estimate: as the client states); authorizations; real job photos; vehicle/uniform photos; payment methods; invoice info.

## Visual direction

Practical and trustworthy: strong contrast, one bold brand color, big tappable buttons, simple icons, sturdy type. Motion minimal. Think "answers in 3 seconds on a cracked phone".

## Imagery

Real jobs and the real person/team beat stock. A clean van/uniform photo builds trust. Avoid generic handshake stock.

## Copy

Plain, local, direct ("Instalator în <city>, ajungem în <interval>" only if true). No exaggerated promises.

## SEO and schema

`Plumber`, `Electrician`, `HVACBusiness`, `RoofingContractor`, `GeneralContractor`, `HouseCleaning`, `MovingCompany`, or `HomeAndConstructionBusiness`; `areaServed`, telephone, hours. Service pages per main service + city if the client wants more SEO.

## Compliance

Authorizations required for the trade (electrical, gas) **[unverified]**: show only what the client provides. Guarantee wording must match their terms. Privacy for forms.

## Worth adding / not

Worth: sticky call bar, WhatsApp with photos, areas served list, simple quote request via WhatsApp, emergency block. Not: fake "online tracking", chat bots.

## Anti-patterns

Phone hidden below the fold; stock "handymen"; made-up certifications; "24/7" if not true; long forms.

## Definition of done

Call button works on every screen; areas and services accurate; real photos; page loads fast on a weak phone; NAP identical to Google Business Profile.
